About

Aben Sojan is a BCA (Honours) student at Mar Augusthinose College, Ramapuram, affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam — currently pursuing his degree, 2025 to 2029. He builds real, live products while studying.

He is the Founder & Developer of Axofy, a serverless SMM platform running live on Vercel, and the builder of MacHub, a student portal for his college. What he loves building most is premium looking web applications — Apple inspired, modern in style — with strong backend work behind them.

At college he is Technical Lead of NEXUS, the BCA department association, and Magazine Editor of the College Student Council for 2026. He is open to internships and to freelance website projects for clients.
